# Scene storyboard

## Core arc

The page follows one red signal from transmission to national-scale infrastructure. Scenes share a state machine instead of drawing unrelated accents.

| Scene | Visual beat | Signal state | Transition out |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Loader | Technical system boots; line sweeps once | `HERO_TRANSMIT` | Line exits right edge |
| Hero | Sunrise tower, antenna lights, RF rings | `HERO_TRANSMIT` | One pulse separates from the tower |
| Company | Abstract Nepal topology, editorial statement | `MAP_ROUTE` | Pulse becomes a route |
| Capabilities | Five disciplines change the environment | `MAP_ROUTE` | Route gains structural geometry |
| Telecom | Survey, foundation, tower, equipment, power, live | `TELECOM_BUILD` | Live signal leaves the mast |
| RF | Drive-test route, nodes, coverage field, sample metrics | `RF_DRIVE` | Route tightens into a single beam |
| Fiber | Macro cable, beam, splice/test/connect sequence | `FIBER_LIGHT` | Beam exits the cable |
| Energy | Beam becomes current, then powers a solar array | `ENERGY_CURRENT` | Panel grid flattens into nodes |
| Digital | Grid becomes software and systems topology | `DIGITAL_NETWORK` | Nodes converge toward the mark |
| Projects / CTA | Real field proof, scale, clients, final identity | `FINAL_CONVERGENCE` | Signal reaches Pie Square |

## Editorial rhythm

Use long, quiet holds for photography and typography. Reserve fast motion for the signal handoffs: hero departure, telecom activation, fiber exit, and final convergence. Project proof should slow down and feel documentary after the abstract sequences.
