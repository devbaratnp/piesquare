# Styleframes

## Shared direction

Industrial editorial direction: carbon black, deep graphite, warm infrastructure white, steel gray, and one controlled signal red. The display type is oversized and tight; technical labels use a mono face. Layouts use open negative space, hard edges, fine rules, and restrained grain. Avoid rounded SaaS panels, blue neon, fake holograms, and decorative motion without a signal meaning.

## SF01 Hero

H01 fills the viewport. The tower sits left of center; the right half holds the title and a thin technical footer. A faint grid sits behind the image. The line begins as a compact radio pulse near the tower and leaves through the right edge. The next scene inherits a short, bright route segment.

## SF02 Telecom construction

The same tower position is built through six labeled phases. A survey grid and low foundation begin as thin lines. Steel layers rise in measured increments. Equipment, power, and signal lights appear late. Keep the photo dark enough for labels, with the red line as the only saturated accent.

## SF03 RF engineering

Near-black field with a large route diagram, sparse nodes, a red coverage sweep, and a small mono readout explicitly labeled `VISUALIZATION`. The map is abstract and non-political. The moving route is the same signal that left the tower.

## SF04 Fiber macro

F01 enters as a close, quiet material shot. Typography sits in the dark area away from the red pulse. The pulse travels through the glass core, exits the image, and becomes an SVG route beneath the metric `2240+ KM`.

## SF05 Project proof

Use the copied company photographs at large scale with consistent crops, muted grade, small source labels, and factual project captions. Let the evidence feel less polished than the cinematic stills while keeping the layout precise.

## SF06 Solar

S01 uses a wide architectural crop. The current line crosses the panel structure once, then becomes a slow grid shimmer. Warm solar gold is limited to the environment; red remains the active state indicator.

## SF07 Final CTA

Return to carbon black. Sparse topology lines gather toward a thin Pie Square mark. The headline is large, left aligned, and partly cropped near the bottom. Buttons are rectangular, high-contrast, and quiet.
